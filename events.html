Turn-Key Hosted Experiences

We turn your venue, party room, or gathering space into an interactive event space—tabletop lounges, live trivia, music bingo, and bracket tournaments hosted start to finish by our Game Connectors. No rulebooks to read, no setup for your group to manage. Pick a format below and we'll bring the rest!

01 SOCIAL MIXERS & GAME LOUNGES

Low-pressure formats built for casual mingling across groups of any size.

Mobile Board Game Lounge

A full lounge built into your space, stocked with 80+ curated titles. Our Game Connectors seat groups by vibe and teach the rules on the spot.

Live Custom Trivia Night

Pub-style trivia with a live emcee covering pop culture, history, and general knowledge—plus a custom round built around your group, party theme, or inside jokes!

Room-Wide Party Games

Easy-to-learn crowd favorites that scale cleanly to a full room:

  • Herd Mentality: Match the majority answer.
  • Hues & Cues: Clue-giving with precise color shades.
  • Blank Slate: Fill in the word your team is thinking.

02 BINGO EXPERIENCES

High-energy, zero-learning-curve games that get the entire room involved at once.

Music Bingo

Numbers become song snippets! Cross off matching hits with a wide variety of games featuring different decades, music genres, themed playlists, etc.

Bingo

Fast-paced traditional or themed bingo rounds complete with high-energy live calls, interactive gameplay, and hosted prize giveaways.

Icebreaker: People Bingo

Custom cards filled with unique attributes ("speaks 2 languages," "has traveled to 5+ countries"). Guests hunt down matches across the room to break the ice.

Team-Based Knowledge Tournaments

Hitster

Wits & Wagers

Fast-Paced Elimination Tournaments

TENZI

Rotating Group Mixers

Bunco
